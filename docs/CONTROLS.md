# Controls

Every player chooses a preset in the lobby with **Y** or **Tab**. Each player's choice is saved.
**X** or **R** in the lobby opens that player's controls screen, where the six digital actions can
be rebound for keyboard and for gamepad. The lobby card, and a reminder at the start of each race,
describe the preset that player picked.

Accelerate and Turbo are always separate buttons:
* **Accelerate** is analog on the trigger. Releasing it engine-brakes, like the NES.
* **Turbo** gives more speed and acceleration, but heats the engine. Holding it long enough always
  overheats you.

## Classic (screen-relative, like the NES)
| | Gamepad | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Lane up (toward the far side) | D-pad Up, left stick up | Up arrow / W |
| Lane down (near side) | D-pad Down, left stick down | Down arrow / S |
| Nose up / wheelie (lean back) | D-pad Left, left stick left | Left arrow / A |
| Nose down (lean forward) | D-pad Right, left stick right | Right arrow / D |
| Accelerate | A (bottom face), RT | X / J / Space |
| Turbo | X (left face), RB | Z / K / Left Shift |
| Pause | Start / Menu | Esc / P |

A lane tap moves exactly one lane. Holding it keeps moving lane by lane.

## Modern (rider-relative)
| | Gamepad | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Steer across the track | left stick left/right (analog), D-pad Left/Right | Left/Right arrows, A/D |
| Lean back / nose up | left stick back, D-pad Down | Down arrow / S |
| Lean forward / nose down | left stick forward, D-pad Up | Up arrow / W |
| Accelerate | RT (analog), A | X / J / Space |
| Turbo | RB, X | Z / K / Left Shift |
| Pause | Start / Menu | Esc / P |

Steering is analog, with light magnetism toward the nearest lane centre.

## What the pitch controls do
* **On the ground:** lean back lifts the front wheel. Wheelie over the small barriers, because
  hitting one fast with the front wheel down crashes you. Keep holding back with the throttle on
  and the bike loops out.
* **In the air:**
  * **Lean back / nose up** floats higher and longer but loses speed, so you land shorter.
  * **Lean forward / nose down** flies lower and farther without losing speed.
  * Either way, bring the nose back so the bike **matches the slope you land on**. The landing meter
    over your bike shows your angle against the green clean window.
* **Landings:**
  * Perfect or clean landings keep your speed. A perfect landing on a down-slope adds speed.
  * Sloppy landings bounce and cost speed.
  * Landing far off the slope's angle crashes you. After a crash, **mash Accelerate** to remount
    sooner.

## Menus
Menus use the D-pad or left stick (keyboard: arrows or WASD). **A / Enter** confirms, **B / Esc**
goes back, **Start / P** starts. In the lobby, **LB/RB (Q/E)** change the AI count.

## Local multiplayer devices
* Press **A** (or **Enter** on the keyboard) on any device in the lobby to join. Every device drives
  only its own rider.
* Four gamepads work, and so does one keyboard plus three gamepads.
* If a controller disconnects mid-race, the race pauses. Reconnect it, or press **A** on a free
  controller to take over that rider.
